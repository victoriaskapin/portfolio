LauTeX Add-on

Render LaTeX equations directly inside your Google Slides.

Get it on Google Workspace Marketplace

About

LauTeX helps you seamlessly insert mathematical equations and symbols into your presentations without leaving Google Slides. Powered by MathJax, the add-on converts your LaTeX code into high-quality images or SVGs.

Features

  • Insert LaTeX equations directly from a sidebar.
  • Edit previously inserted equations with a single click.
  • Powered by MathJax for high-quality rendering.
  • No data collection – your code stays on your slide.
